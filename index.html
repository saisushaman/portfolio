    EP.03deep dive

    How I get
    things done

    Seven questions. Scroll to fly to each one, then open its live tool.

    01 · THINK

    How I think through an engineering problem

    Problem first, tech second. I separate hard constraints from preferences, score at least three options and write the decision down so anyone can challenge it.

    02 · DESIGN

    How I design AI architectures

    Small, swappable parts: gateway, router, retrieval or agent, model, guardrails. Tracing and evals from day one, and every request takes the cheapest path that answers it well.

    03 · AGENTS

    How my agents work

    Plan, act, observe, verify. Every step is a traced span with tokens and cost, and nothing that writes happens without a human yes.

    04 · AUTOMATE

    How I automate work with agents

    Node-based workflows where agents do the busywork: an orchestrator splits the job across specialist agents, a critic checks their output, and a person approves before anything goes out.

    05 · EVALUATE

    How I evaluate model performance

    A golden test set from real cases, an eval gate on every release, and benchmarks on my own tasks. Never vibes.

    06 · SAFETY

    How I handle failures, security and hallucinations

    Defence in depth: injection and PII screening, grounded answers or “I don’t know”, output checks, approval for actions, graceful fallbacks.

    07 · SHIP

    How I take an idea from requirements to deployment

    Acceptance criteria before code, small reviewed slices, a gated pipeline, canary rollouts with automatic rollback, and monitoring that feeds the next plan.
